> **Title:** PR #207 (`bootstrap_poll_allowed`): the PEEKDATA snapshot is not re-checked after resume — measured race (recalibrated), question on impact
>
> Live state (checked 2026-11-06): #207 Open at `e7f5f552`.
>
> The gate: `bootstrap_poll_allowed` (channel_hooks.rs:13–37) decides SYS_POLL from a PEEKDATA snapshot: >16 fds ⇒ deny, fd > 2 ⇒ deny, PEEKDATA failure ⇒ deny. The kernel copies the pollfd array from live tracee memory at execution; the validated values are never re-read.
>
> Measured with a standalone two-thread harness replicating the gate, not #207's binary. An earlier harness version reported ~170k–183k hits per 1M polls — contaminated by inherited runner stdin; those magnitudes are withdrawn. The recalibrated harness gives the tracee an open, empty pipe as fd 0; three controls pass (fixed fd ⇒ zero events; fixed sentinel ⇒ 100% POLLNVAL; fixed real fd ⇒ 100% POLLHUP). On ubuntu-24.04, two 1M-poll runs: the snapshot validated fd 0 while the kernel polled the sentinel 3,704 times (0.74% executed), and real fd 3 — never allowed — 3,091 times (0.62%). Gate-loop median ~3.3 µs. Harness and numbers: https://github.com/hafid-logic/ginux-watch/blob/8f855b7d6c91ad901e618c96fb1ce960607e3e60/poc/poc207_t123.c, https://github.com/hafid-logic/ginux-watch/blob/8f855b7d6c91ad901e618c96fb1ce960607e3e60/inbox/evidence/2026-11-06-222-evidence/04-poc207-recalibration.md
>
> Limits: our supervisor's window is wider than ginux's SYSEMU design; rates are harness-specific; whether a ginux tracee can ever hold fd > 2 stays open — if it holds, the gate is defense-in-depth with a harmless POLLNVAL worst case.
>
> Question: is fd > 2 the only intended guarantee, or would a cheap re-validation after the last PEEKDATA — or a doc-comment noting the snapshot is not re-checked — be acceptable?

1180:**No kernel tracing, no nested KVM.** The guest kernel has the eBPF syscall and JIT (root only: unprivileged BPF is off, so use `sudo`), and socket, cgroup and XDP programs load. What it lacks is the tracing side: no kprobes, no ftrace or tracepoints, and no BTF (`/sys/kernel/btf/vmlinux` is absent), so `bpftrace`, BCC tools and `perf probe` do not work and there is nothing to attach a tracing program to. There is also no `/dev/kvm` in the guest (the kernel is a KVM guest, not a KVM host), so Firecracker, QEMU with KVM, Docker-in-VM runtimes that need it, and nested microVMs cannot run; QEMU in pure emulation (`-accel tcg`) works, slowly.
